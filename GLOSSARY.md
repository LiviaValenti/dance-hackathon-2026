# Dance Hackathon 2026

A personal dance-learning app for hobby dancers, whether or not they have a dance studio. It compares you with a reference dance on your phone, places you at a level and coaches you forward with a plan made for you.

## Language

### People

**Dancer**:
The person using the app to learn to dance. Either a Studio dancer or a Solo dancer.
_Avoid_: User, student, member

**Studio dancer**:
A hobby Dancer who takes classes at a dance studio and uses the app to practise between classes.
_Avoid_: Studio member, elev

**Solo dancer**:
A hobby Dancer with no studio, usually because there are few or no dance services where they live, who learns with the app alone.
_Avoid_: Home dancer, self-learner, independent dancer

### Learning

**Reference**:
The recording of a dance or move done the way it should be done, which a Dancer learns from and is compared against.
_Avoid_: Baseline, reference video, referansevideo, template

**Choreography**:
A sequence of moves set to music that a Dancer learns as a whole, split into Parts.
_Avoid_: Routine, dance, koreografi

**Part**:
A short, continuous piece of a Choreography that is learned and practised on its own before being combined with the others.
_Avoid_: Segment, chunk, del, bit

**Plan**:
The ordered list of Parts and Choreographies a Dancer works through, chosen from their Level, goals, time and aches, and adjusted after each Attempt.
_Avoid_: Program, course, lesson plan, curriculum

**Level**:
Where a Dancer stands in a given style, from beginner upwards, with the body areas that are strong and weak. An estimate, not a grade.
_Avoid_: Skill, rank, grade, nivå

**Level check**:
A short placement where the Dancer first says what Level they think they are, then dances one move at that Level with the camera, and the app confirms or adjusts the Level.
_Avoid_: Level test, placement test, assessment, nivåtest

### Practice

**Attempt**:
One recording of the Dancer dancing a Part or Choreography, analysed against its Reference.
_Avoid_: Try, take, forsøk (and not "session": a practice session can hold many Attempts)

**Score**:
How closely an Attempt matches its Reference, 0–100, overall and per body area. Private to the Dancer.
_Avoid_: Grade, rating, points

**Ghost**:
The Dancer's own previous Attempt at the same Part, which a new Attempt is compared with. Dancers are compared only with themselves, never with each other.
_Avoid_: Ghost runner, personal best, previous score

### Coaching

**Mascot**:
The character that greets, guides and encourages the Dancer, dances to the beat, and whose voice the Coach speaks in. Currently Taktvekteren ("keeper of the beat"): a watchman with a staff and a lantern, inspired by (but not) the Gjensidige logo figure. Replaceable: its name, look and tone are settings, not part of any screen.
_Avoid_: Leo, danseløven, Vekteren, the Wanderer, the Gjensidige man, logo

**Tempo**:
The beat of a Reference's music, in beats per minute. The Mascot dances to it, and it slows down with the Reference when a Dancer practises at reduced speed.
_Avoid_: BPM (in prose), rhythm, takt

**Coach**:
The AI that turns an analysed Attempt and the Plan's next step into personal, encouraging feedback with a growth mindset. It speaks through the Mascot. It explains the next step but does not choose it, and it never diagnoses.
_Avoid_: AI coach, chatbot, assistant
