# Pitch: decisions and script

From the grill session on 2026-11-12. The pitch is 2 minutes, followed by a 3-minute demo ([plan.md](plan.md)). Terms as in [GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md).

The deck has moved on since this script was written. For the current wording, use the speaker notes in [pitch-deck/](pitch-deck/); the sources and Q&A answers here still apply.

## Setting

- Judges: Janus, Johan, Kim, Kristine, Morten and René. Their question: "If this were a real startup, would we invest?" Criteria: customer value, ability to execute, innovation, pitch and demo, answers to the judges' questions.
- The audience votes at hackfest.live/vote (audience prize). Upload the deck as a PDF.
- The pitch and the slides are in Norwegian; the slides carry short text and numbers.
- When cutting for time, business beats audience engagement.

## Script (about 2:00)

Three team members dance with coffee cups. The pitcher reads the voice-over live into a handheld mic. About 240 words of speech (about 1:40 at 145 words per minute) plus about 8 s of dance and pauses.

**Opening (0:00–0:35)**
> *[Music, three dance with coffee cups for about 8 s. The music cuts.]*
> Husk sist du sto på dansegulvet med noe i hånda.
> **Dancer (looks at the cup):** Det er kaffe.
> Tenkte du da at du ble atten prosent smartere? *[slide: 18 %]* At du som åttiåring klarer å ta på deg sokkene selv? *[slide: 73 %]* Neppe. Men dans er bra for deg.
> Rekk opp hånda hvis du har danset hjemme i stua siden sist du var ute på byen. *[few hands]* Vi elsker å danse. Vi gjør det bare nesten aldri.

**Problem and injuries (0:35–0:55)**
> For mange er det altfor flaut. Der folk bor, finnes det ofte ingen dansetilbud. Og YouTube sier ikke hva du gjør feil.
> Og dans er idrett: tre av fire skader hos amatører er belastningsskader. For mye, for fort, feil teknikk.

**Solution (0:55–1:15)**
> Så vi har laget en dansecoach i telefonen. Den ser deg, men ingen andre gjør det. Den finner nivået ditt, lager en plan og sakker ned når det går for fort. Den heter Taktvekteren. *[pause]* Han minner kanskje om noen dere kjenner.

**Business (1:15–1:40)**
> *[Market slide]* Bare i Norge danser rundt 140 000 på hobbynivå. Og Europa har 45 000 dansestudioer. Det er kundene våre. *[Click]*
> Dansere får tre analyser gratis i måneden, så Premium. Studioer betaler for å legge ut ukas koreografi, se hvor elevene sliter og bli anbefalt til dansere i nærheten. For når du er klar til å komme deg ut av stua, skal studioet stå klart. Og dansere og lærere bygger merkevaren sin her, med følgere og merkevareavtaler.

**Closing and hand-off (1:40–2:00)**
> Samme motor kan se om bokseren slipper garden, om skuddet har riktig vinkel, og hvordan klatreren når lenger. Men målet er ikke stua. Målet er dansegulvet.
> Om litt møter dere Ingrid. Hun bor en time fra nærmeste studio.

## Facts and sources

- **18 %:** Coventry University, 27 amateur dancers, 30 minutes of salsa, better reaction ability and visual memory. [forskning.no, 2020](https://www.forskning.no/norges-forskningsrad/kan-man-danse-seg-smartere/1735461). Small study, no control group described; "neppe" covers it.
- **73 %:** Osuka et al. 2018 (Scand J Med Sci Sports, doi 10.1111/sms.13336), 1,003 women aged 75–84 in Tokyo followed for 8 years; dancers had 73 % lower risk of needing help with daily tasks such as dressing. [forskning.no, 2019](https://www.forskning.no/aldring-trening/kan-dansing-hjelpe-eldre-med-a-kle-pa-seg/1276208). Observational: an association, not proof of cause.
- **3 in 4 overuse:** Vassallo et al. 2019, systematic review: amateur dancers about 1 injury per 1,000 dance hours; about 75 % of their injuries are overuse. [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/329223900_Injury_rates_and_characteristics_in_recreational_elite_student_and_professional_dancers_A_systematic_review). Check the full paper before the slide is final.
- Backup for Q&A:
  - Smith et al. 2015: amateur ballet 0.97 injuries per 1,000 hours. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1177/2325967115592621)
  - Hincapié et al. 2008: professionals, 84 % injured in their lifetime, 42 % in six months. [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225295665_Musculoskeletal_Injuries_and_Pain_in_Dancers_A_Systematic_Review)
  - Fredriksen & Clarsen: Norwegian National Ballet, 64 % injured on average at any time (preliminary, 57 dancers). [BJSM abstract](https://bibliotheek.ehb.be:2102/10.1136/bjsports-2014-093494.97)
  - Other sports are roughly 1–5 injuries per 1,000 hours; studies define injury differently. [PMC7534944](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7534944)

- **Market** (from `datagrunnlag_dans.xlsx`): dance studios Norway ~175 (150 registered), Nordics ~1,200, Europe ~45,000 (46,245 registered, April 2026), global ~145,000 (~129,300 registered in 61 countries); [SmartScrapers](https://rentechdigital.com/smartscraper/business-listings/dance-schools). Dancers in Norway: hobby ~140,000 (100–180k), active ~45,000 (30–60k), pro/pre-pro ~1,750 (1–2.5k); working estimates, not official statistics; levels overlap and must not be summed. Documented minimum: Norges Danseforbund 16,514 members (2025).

- **Revenue** (from `inntjeningsmodell_dans.xlsx`): prices Premium 999 kr/year, analysis packs 200 kr/buyer/year, studio licence 12,000 kr/year. Norway, yearly gross: cautious 1.1 MNOK (2 % Premium, 0.5 % hobby buyers, 5 % of studios), base 3.1 MNOK (5 %, 2 %, 15 %), strong 6.5 MNOK (10 %, 5 %, 30 %). Global sensitivity, Premium only: 0.1 % of 45 M active dancers = 45 MNOK/year. Contribution per Premium customer after VAT, payment, video and any studio share: about 330–745 kr/year, before salaries and marketing. Gross revenue, a scenario analysis, not a forecast. The global main scenarios (1.2–7 billion NOK) are left off the slide on purpose: they assume 2–10 % of all active dancers worldwide.

## Rules for wording

- Never say the app "prevents" injury ([ADR 0004](adr/0004-rules-decide-coach-phrases.md), EU MDR). Q&A answer: "Vi lover ingenting medisinsk, men vi bygger det som fagfolk anbefaler: gradvis progresjon og riktig teknikk."
- Taktvekteren is inspired by, but is not, the Gjensidige logo figure. The logo never goes in the deck.

## Business model

| Who | Model |
|---|---|
| Dancers | Freemium: 3 analysed Attempts free per month, unlimited with Premium |
| Studios | Tiers: Distribution (post choreographies to your students), Insight (see where students struggle), Visibility (recommended to dancers nearby) |
| Creators | Dancers and teachers build a brand, gain followers, brand deals |

Studios use the app as a pull product and marketing: Solo dancers who learn in the app find a studio when they are ready to leave the living room.

## Q&A preparation

- **Studio insight versus privacy:** studios see aggregated, anonymous patterns ("70 % of your students struggle with the turn in Part 3"), never individuals or video. Only scores leave the phone, only with the Dancer's consent, and only in aggregate.
- **Accounts:** they come with Premium and studios; video still stays on the phone. The demo has no accounts because the free core needs none ([ADR 0003](adr/0003-no-accounts-studio-content-bundled.md)).
- **Distribution:** "Partnere som vil ha aktive kunder eller ansatte, som forsikringsselskap og bedriftshelse, kan tilby Premium som en fordel."
- Pitch a standalone startup, not a Gjensidige product; the judges judge as themselves. Taktvekteren is the only nod. Do not address judges personally from stage.
- Expect questions on growth and brand partnerships, technology and privacy, injury claims, and day-to-day customer experience.

## Closing message

Dancing in the living room is fine, but the goal is for people to enjoy dancing and moving their bodies, out on the dance floor. The app is the way in.

Later ambition: the same engine for more sports and exercise. Boxing (dropping your guard too often), football (the angle that makes a shot hard and efficient), bouldering (how to angle the body to reach further), rehabilitation (doing the physiotherapist's exercises correctly), yoga (hip angle in warrior pose), golf (shoulder rotation in the swing).

Rehabilitation is the most sensitive: a product that guides physiotherapy exercises must be assessed against the EU MDR and built with physiotherapists. On stage it is an ambition, never a claim.
